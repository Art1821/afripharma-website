1. WHAT ARE COOKIES?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember your preferences and improve your browsing experience.

2. HOW WE USE COOKIES

AfriPharma Health Systems Ltd uses minimal cookies on https://afripharma-health.com to ensure basic functionality and understand how visitors use our Website. We are committed to privacy-first practices.

3. TYPES OF COOKIES WE USE

A. ESSENTIAL COOKIES (Necessary)

These cookies are required for the Website to function properly: session management, security features, and website navigation. Duration: Session-based (deleted when you close your browser)

B. ANALYTICS COOKIES (Optional)

We use privacy-focused analytics tools (Plausible) to collect anonymized data: page views, referral sources, geographic location (country-level only), and device type.

What we DO NOT collect: Personal identifiers, IP addresses, individual user tracking, cross-site tracking data, or advertising profiles.

4. COOKIES WE DO NOT USE

AfriPharma does NOT use: advertising cookies, social media tracking cookies, third-party marketing cookies, or cross-site tracking cookies.

5. ANALYTICS PROVIDER

We use Plausible Analytics: GDPR-compliant, no personal data collected, no cookies requiring consent under GDPR, open-source and transparent, EU-based infrastructure.

Learn more: https://plausible.io/privacy

6. YOUR COOKIE CHOICES

You can control cookies through your browser settings or opt out of analytics by using browser "Do Not Track" settings (we respect DNT signals).
