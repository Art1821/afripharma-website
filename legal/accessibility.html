Our Commitment

AfriPharma Health Systems is committed to providing a website that is accessible to the widest possible audience, regardless of technology or ability. We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA.

Conformance Status

We are actively working toward full WCAG 2.1 AA conformance. Based on ongoing manual reviews and automated checks, the site is currently partially conformant; certain components are being improved to meet contrast, keyboard navigation, and ARIA labeling best practices.

Measures We Take

  • Designing for semantic HTML structure and logical heading order
  • Providing text alternatives for meaningful images
  • Maintaining sufficient color contrast for text and UI components
  • Ensuring keyboard operability and visible focus styles
  • Using descriptive link text and form labels
  • Testing with automated tools (e.g., Lighthouse, axe) and manual checks
  • Continuous iteration as content and features evolve

Compatibility with Browsers & Assistive Technology

This site is designed to work with the last two major versions of modern browsers (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari) and with common assistive technologies on desktop and mobile. You’ll get the best experience by keeping your browser and operating system updated.

Known Limitations

We’re tracking and addressing the following:

  • Occasional missing ARIA roles on new components
  • Minor contrast issues on legacy text elements
  • Keyboard focus order refinements on specific interactive elements

If you find an issue not listed here, please let us know.

Feedback & Support

We welcome your feedback on the accessibility of this website. If you encounter accessibility barriers or need content in an alternative format, contact us:

  • Email: info@afripharma-health.com
  • Response time: We aim to respond within 2 business days

Assessment Approach

AfriPharma assesses accessibility through internal self-evaluation, code reviews, and automated scanning, supplemented by periodic manual testing.

Ongoing Improvements

Accessibility is an ongoing effort. As we add new pages and features, we will continue to iterate toward full WCAG 2.1 AA conformance. Your feedback helps us prioritize fixes that matter most.
