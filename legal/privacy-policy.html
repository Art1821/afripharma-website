Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 26, 2024

1. INTRODUCTION

AfriPharma Health Systems Ltd ("AfriPharma", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you visit our website https://afripharma-health.com (the "Website").

AfriPharma is a Mauritius-based entity being formalized under the Mauritius Financial Services Commission (FSC) as a Global Business Company (GBC). This policy is aligned with the Data Protection Act 2017 of Mauritius and incorporates principles from the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) where applicable.

2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

We collect minimal information for the following purposes:

2.1 Contact Information

When you submit an inquiry via our contact form, we collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Company name (optional)
  • Message content
  • Phone number (if provided)

2.2 Website Analytics

We use privacy-focused analytics (Plausible) to collect anonymized data:

  • Page views
  • Referral sources
  • Geographic location (country-level only)
  • Device type (desktop/mobile)

We do NOT collect:

  • Personal identifiers
  • IP addresses
  • Individual user tracking data
  • Cookies for advertising purposes

3. PURPOSE OF DATA COLLECTION

We process your data solely for:

  • Responding to investor and partnership inquiries
  • Regulatory and compliance communications
  • Website performance optimization
  • Providing information about our pharmaceutical manufacturing project

4. LEGAL BASIS FOR PROCESSING

We process your data based on:

  • Legitimate interest: responding to business inquiries
  • Consent: when you submit information via contact forms
  • Legal obligation: regulatory compliance under Mauritius law

5. DATA RETENTION

  • Contact form submissions: 24 months maximum
  • Analytics data: 12 months (anonymized)
  • Investor communications: As required by regulatory obligations

After retention periods expire, data is securely deleted.

6. DATA SHARING AND THIRD PARTIES

We do NOT share, sell, or rent your personal data to third parties.

Limited exceptions:

  • Legal compliance: if required by Mauritius or international law
  • Service providers: minimal data shared with essential service providers (email hosting, website infrastructure) under strict data protection agreements

Third-party services we use:

  • Website hosting - GDPR-compliant
  • Email hosting - ISO 27001 certified
  • Plausible Analytics - EU-based, privacy-focused

7. YOUR RIGHTS

Under Mauritius Data Protection Act 2017, you have the right to:

  • Access: Request copies of your personal data
  • Rectification: Correct inaccurate information
  • Erasure: Request deletion of your data
  • Restriction: Limit how we use your data
  • Objection: Object to processing based on legitimate interest
  • Data portability: Receive your data in a portable format

To exercise these rights, contact: privacy@afripharma-health.com

8. DATA SECURITY

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data:

  • HTTPS encryption on all website pages
  • Secure data storage with access controls
  • Regular security audits
  • Two-factor authentication (2FA) for admin access
  • Minimal data collection practices

9. COOKIES AND TRACKING

This website uses minimal cookies:

Essential Cookies: Session management (required for website functionality)

Analytics Cookies: Anonymous traffic analysis via Plausible (no personal data collected, no cross-site tracking, no advertising purposes)

You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though this may affect website functionality. For more details, see our Cookies Notice.

10. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Our website is not directed at individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

11. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the Website after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

12. SUPERVISORY AUTHORITY

If you have concerns about our data practices, you may contact:

Data Protection Office
Ministry of Information Technology, Communication and Innovation
Republic of Mauritius
Email: dpo@govmu.org

13. CONTACT US

For privacy-related inquiries:

Email: privacy@afripharma-health.com
Website: https://afripharma-health.com/contact
