Regional Expansion

From Zimbabwe
to the SADC Region.

AfriPharma's distribution footprint is being built market by market — with Zimbabwe as the operational anchor and Botswana, South Africa, and Zambia in the pipeline.

Zimbabwe — Phase 1

Primary market. MCAZ WDP application active. Harare DC in Msasa under development. Stanbic identified as primary banking partner.

Botswana — Phase 1

Entity incorporated April 2026. BURS TIN registered. BoMRA compliance framework prepared. Banking package in progress.

South Africa — Phase 2

SAHPRA-aligned compliance preparation underway. Entity formation planned. Tinashe Chinoingira leading South Africa technical and supply chain development.

Zambia — Phase 3

ZDA engagement active. ZAMRA and ZAMSA introduction documents prepared. Ministry of Health and Ministry of Commerce and Trade outreach in progress.
