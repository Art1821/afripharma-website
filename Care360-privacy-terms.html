AfriPharma360™ Privacy & Terms

Effective Date: June 2026

1. Nature of Service

AfriPharma360™ is a healthcare coordination and family support service operated by AfriPharma Zimbabwe (Pvt) Ltd (“AfriPharma”, “we”, “us”). AfriPharma360™ assists families through healthcare navigation, medication coordination support, appointment coordination, wellness communication, family reporting, and related healthcare coordination activities.

AfriPharma360™ subscription fees cover coordination and support services only. Subscription fees do not include prescription medications, OTC products, pharmacy charges, delivery fees, medical consultations, laboratory services, hospital services, or other healthcare products or services.

AfriPharma360™ is not an emergency medical service, health insurance product, medical practice, clinical diagnosis service, pharmacy service, prescription fulfillment service, or replacement for direct medical care.

2. Medical Disclaimer

AfriPharma360™ does not diagnose medical conditions, prescribe medications, sell medications, dispense medicines, provide medical treatment, fulfill prescriptions, or make clinical decisions on behalf of patients.

All medical decisions remain the responsibility of the patient and their licensed healthcare providers.

3. No Sale or Dispensing of Medication

AfriPharma360™ does not sell, prescribe, dispense, distribute, or fulfill prescription medications through its subscription service.

Prescription medications, OTC products, pharmacy services, consultations, laboratory services, delivery services, and other healthcare products or services are provided separately by licensed healthcare providers, pharmacies, facilities, or approved service partners, and may require separate payment arrangements.

4. Emergency Situations

In a medical emergency, patients should immediately contact local emergency services or proceed directly to the nearest hospital, clinic, or licensed healthcare facility.

AfriPharma360™ cannot dispatch emergency responders and should not be relied upon for emergency medical care.

5. Information We Collect

AfriPharma360™ may collect:

  • Sponsor contact information
  • Patient identification information
  • Location and address information
  • Medication information
  • Health conditions and allergy information
  • Emergency contact information
  • Communication preferences
  • Appointment coordination details where applicable
  • Billing and subscription information

6. Use of Information

Information is used solely for:

  • Providing AfriPharma360™ coordination and family support services
  • Communicating with sponsors and patients
  • Supporting medication coordination activities
  • Supporting appointment coordination activities
  • Providing family updates and wellness communications
  • Managing subscriptions and billing
  • Compliance and quality assurance activities

7. Cross-Border Data Processing

Because AfriPharma360™ supports diaspora families, information may be processed, stored, transmitted, or accessed across multiple jurisdictions including Zimbabwe, the United States, and other countries where sponsors reside.

8. Consent & Authorization — Patient, Sponsor, and POA

AfriPharma360™ is designed to support both sponsors and patients. In most cases, we require:

  • Patient consent to receive coordination support; and
  • Patient consent to share updates with named family members or sponsors.

If a patient cannot provide informed consent, such as in cases involving dementia, services may be provided based on a legally authorized representative / Power of Attorney or equivalent authority, with appropriate documentation.

9. Privacy Commitment

AfriPharma does not sell personal or health-related information.

Access to information is restricted to authorized personnel involved in delivering AfriPharma360™ services, quality oversight, compliance activities, and customer support.

10. Sharing of Information

We may share limited information with the following parties only as necessary to provide AfriPharma360™ services:

  • Licensed healthcare providers and facilities for coordination and continuity of care
  • Licensed pharmacies and delivery partners solely to support coordination of prescription refills, delivery requests, and continuity of care where authorized by the patient or legally authorized representative
  • Payment processors such as Stripe and other approved billing providers solely for processing AfriPharma360™ subscription fees, subscription management, invoicing, and related customer support activities

11. Communication Channels — WhatsApp / Email / Phone

AfriPharma360™ may communicate with sponsors and patients via WhatsApp, email, phone, or other agreed channels for convenience and timely coordination.

If you prefer not to use WhatsApp or would like alternative communication arrangements, you may request this during onboarding or by contacting us.

12. Security

We implement administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal and health-related information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse. Access is limited to authorized personnel on a need-to-know basis.

13. Retention

We retain information only as long as needed to provide services, meet legal and compliance obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce these terms. We then securely delete or anonymize information where appropriate.

14. Your Rights & Requests

You may request to access, correct, or delete your information, subject to legal and operational retention requirements.

15. Billing & Subscription Terms

AfriPharma360™ subscriptions are billed monthly through Stripe and other approved payment providers.

Subscription fees cover healthcare coordination, communication, family reporting, wellness support, appointment coordination, medication coordination support, and related non-clinical services.

Subscription fees do not include prescription medications, OTC products, pharmacy charges, delivery fees, medical consultations, laboratory services, hospital services, emergency medical services, or any other healthcare products or services provided by third parties.

Such products and services may require separate payment arrangements with the applicable provider, pharmacy, facility, or service partner.

Subscriptions automatically renew until cancelled by the subscriber.

16. Cancellation Policy

Subscribers may cancel at any time through the customer billing portal or by contacting:

care360@afripharma-health.com

Services may continue until the end of the current paid billing period.

17. Service Limitations

Service availability may vary depending on:

  • Geographic location
  • Provider availability
  • Pharmacy availability
  • Transportation conditions
  • Communication infrastructure
  • Local operating conditions
