AfriPharma360™ Privacy & Terms

Effective Date: June 2026

1. Nature of Service

AfriPharma360™ is a healthcare coordination and family support service operated by AfriPharma Health Systems. AfriPharma360™ assists families through healthcare navigation, medication coordination support, appointment support, wellness communication, and related care coordination activities.

AfriPharma360™ is not an emergency medical service, health insurance product, medical practice, clinical diagnosis service, or replacement for direct medical care.

2. Medical Disclaimer

AfriPharma360™ does not diagnose medical conditions, prescribe medications, dispense medicines, provide medical treatment, or make clinical decisions on behalf of patients.

All medical decisions remain the responsibility of the patient and their licensed healthcare providers.

3. Emergency Situations

In a medical emergency, patients should immediately contact local emergency services or proceed directly to the nearest hospital, clinic, or licensed healthcare facility.

AfriPharma360™ cannot dispatch emergency responders and should not be relied upon for emergency medical care.

4. Information We Collect

AfriPharma360™ may collect:

  • Sponsor contact information
  • Patient identification information
  • Location and address information
  • Medication information
  • Health conditions and allergy information
  • Emergency contact information
  • Communication preferences
  • Billing and subscription information

5. Use of Information

Information is used solely for:

  • Providing AfriPharma360™ services
  • Communicating with sponsors and patients
  • Supporting medication coordination activities
  • Supporting appointment coordination activities
  • Providing family updates and wellness communications
  • Managing subscriptions and billing
  • Compliance and quality assurance activities

6. Cross-Border Data Processing

Because AfriPharma360™ supports diaspora families, information may be processed, stored, transmitted, or accessed across multiple jurisdictions including Zimbabwe, the United States, and other countries where sponsors reside.

By enrolling in AfriPharma360™, sponsors consent to the collection, storage, processing, transmission, and cross-border transfer of information necessary to provide the service.

7. Privacy Commitment

AfriPharma Health Systems does not sell personal or health-related information.

Access to information is restricted to authorized personnel involved in delivering AfriPharma360™ services, quality oversight, compliance activities, and customer support.

8. Billing & Subscription Terms

AfriPharma360™ subscriptions are billed monthly through Stripe and other approved payment providers.

Subscriptions automatically renew until cancelled by the subscriber.

9. Cancellation Policy

Subscribers may cancel at any time through the customer billing portal or by contacting:

care360@afripharma-health.com

Services may continue until the end of the current paid billing period.

10. Service Limitations

Service availability may vary depending on:

  • Geographic location
  • Provider availability
  • Pharmacy availability
  • Transportation conditions
  • Communication infrastructure
  • Local operating conditions

11. Changes to Terms

AfriPharma Health Systems reserves the right to update these Terms and Privacy provisions from time to time. Updated versions will be posted on this page.

12. Contact Information

For questions regarding AfriPharma360™ services, privacy practices, or these terms, contact:

care360@afripharma-health.com

Important Service Disclaimer:
AfriPharma360™ is a healthcare coordination and family support service. It does not diagnose medical conditions, prescribe medications, dispense medicines, provide medical treatment, or replace the relationship between a patient and licensed healthcare providers. AfriPharma360™ is not an emergency medical service.
