How AfriPharma360™ Works

AfriPharma360™ gives families a structured way to support loved ones in Zimbabwe through coordinated communication, reminders, follow-up, and healthcare navigation.

1. Enrol

Complete the online enrolment form with sponsor details, patient information, location, medications, and support needs.

2. Verify

An AfriPharma360 coordinator reviews the enrolment, confirms payment, assigns a reference number, and contacts the sponsor.

3. Support

Ongoing support begins according to the selected plan, including medication coordination, appointment support, and family updates.
