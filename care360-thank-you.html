Thank You for Joining AfriPharma Care360™

Your enrollment request and/or payment has been received.

A Care360 Coordinator will contact you within 24 hours to complete onboarding and confirm the details for your loved one.

What Happens Next

  1. We review your Care360 enrollment information.
  2. A Care360 Coordinator contacts you by email or WhatsApp.
  3. We confirm your loved one’s details, location, and support needs.
  4. Your Care360 profile is activated.
  5. Monthly support begins according to your selected plan.

Care360 is a healthcare coordination service and is not an emergency medical service. In a medical emergency, please contact local emergency services or go directly to the nearest hospital.
