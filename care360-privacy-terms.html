Care360 Privacy & Terms

1. Nature of Service

AfriPharma Care360™ is a healthcare coordination and support service. It is not an emergency medical service, insurance product, clinical diagnosis service, or replacement for direct medical care.

2. Medical Disclaimer

Care360 does not diagnose, prescribe, or provide medical treatment. Medical decisions remain between the patient and licensed healthcare providers.

3. Emergency Situations

In a medical emergency, patients must contact local emergency services or proceed directly to the nearest hospital. Care360 cannot dispatch emergency responders.

4. Information Collected

Care360 may collect sponsor information, patient contact information, location details, medication information, known conditions, allergies, and other information required to coordinate services.

5. Use of Information

Information is used only to coordinate Care360 services, communicate with sponsors and patients, support medication and appointment coordination, and provide family updates.

6. Privacy Commitment

AfriPharma does not sell personal or health-related information. Access to Care360 information is limited to personnel involved in providing the service.

7. Billing

Care360 is billed monthly through Stripe or PayPal. Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled.

8. Cancellation

Subscribers may cancel at any time through the customer portal or by contacting care360@afripharma-health.com.

9. Service Limitations

Service availability may vary by location, provider availability, pharmacy availability, transportation conditions, communication access, and local circumstances.

10. Contact

For questions, email care360@afripharma-health.com.
