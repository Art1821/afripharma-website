What We Offer.

Six core pharmacy services — designed to improve medicine access and patient outcomes across our launch markets.

Prescription Fulfillment

Accurate, safe dispensing of prescription medicines — aligned to MCAZ standards, with pharmacist verification on every order.

Medication Counseling

Pharmacist-led counseling to help patients understand their medicines, manage side effects, and maintain adherence to therapy.

Chronic Disease Support

Structured chronic care programs for diabetes, hypertension, asthma, HIV/AIDS, and other long-term conditions — including regular monitoring and adherence support.

Home Delivery

Convenient, trackable medicine delivery to patients' homes — eliminating the need to travel for refills, and ensuring therapy is never interrupted.

Refill Management

Proactive refill scheduling and reminders — ensuring patients on chronic therapy never run out of essential medicines.

OTC & Wellness Products

Quality OTC health and wellness products — including analgesics, vitamins, skincare, and personal health items — available in-store and for delivery.
