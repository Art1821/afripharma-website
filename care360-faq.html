AfriPharma Care360™ FAQ

Answers to common questions about Care360 enrollment, billing, support, and service coverage.

What is Care360?

Care360 is a healthcare coordination service that helps families support loved ones in Zimbabwe through medication coordination, appointment support, family updates, and healthcare navigation.

Who is Care360 for?

Care360 is designed for diaspora families, adult children supporting parents, caregivers, elderly patients, and families who want better visibility into a loved one’s healthcare needs.

Is Care360 health insurance?

No. Care360 is not health insurance. It is a healthcare coordination and support service.

Is Care360 a medical provider?

No. Care360 does not diagnose, prescribe, or provide medical treatment. Medical decisions remain between patients and licensed healthcare providers.

Where is Care360 available?

Care360 is launching initially in Harare and Bulawayo, Zimbabwe. Additional locations will be added over time.

What happens after I enroll?

After enrollment, a Care360 Coordinator reviews your submission and contacts you within 24 business hours to confirm the patient’s information and begin onboarding.

What is included in Essential?

Essential includes Care360 profile creation, medication tracking, refill reminders, WhatsApp support, and monthly family updates.

What is included in Plus?

Plus includes everything in Essential, plus medication coordination, prescription refill management, pharmacy delivery coordination, appointment scheduling support, and priority support.

What is included in Platinum?

Platinum includes everything in Plus, plus a dedicated Care Coordinator, wellness verification calls, enhanced family reporting, appointment coordination, escalation support, and priority delivery support.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Care360 subscriptions may be cancelled at any time through the customer portal or by contacting care360@afripharma-health.com.

What happens in an emergency?

Care360 is not an emergency medical service. In a medical emergency, the patient should contact local emergency services or go directly to the nearest hospital.

How do I contact Care360?

Email us at care360@afripharma-health.com.
