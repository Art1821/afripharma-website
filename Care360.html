Why Afripharma360 Exists

Millions of Africans
Support Family From Abroad.

Whether in the US, UK, Canada, or Australia — African diaspora families often carry the responsibility of coordinating healthcare for parents and loved ones back home. The distance makes it difficult. Reliable, professional local support is hard to find.

Afripharma360 was built specifically to bridge that gap — giving families a trusted, coordinated healthcare support service they can count on, no matter where they are in the world.

What's Covered

What Afripharma360 Provides.

Every Afripharma360 plan is built around the core services families need most — with more support at higher tiers.

Medication Coordination

Prescription tracking, refill reminders, and medication management to ensure your loved one never runs out of essential medicines.

Appointment Support

Scheduling, reminders, and coordination for doctor visits, specialist appointments, and follow-up care.

Home Delivery

Pharmacy delivery coordination to your loved one's home in Harare or Bulawayo — arranged through your coordinator.

Family Reporting

Regular written updates and WhatsApp communication so you always know how your loved one is doing.

Wellness Verification

Scheduled check-in calls and wellness visits to verify your loved one's health status. Platinum tier.

Dedicated Coordinator

A named Care360 coordinator assigned to your patient — a consistent, trusted local point of contact. Platinum tier.

Chronic Care Support

Structured support for diabetes, hypertension, HIV/AIDS, asthma, and other long-term conditions.

Escalation Support

When something needs urgent attention, your coordinator escalates to the right professional or facility. Platinum tier.
