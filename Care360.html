Why AfriPharma360 Exists

Millions of Africans Support Family From Abroad.

Whether in the US, UK, Canada, or Australia — African diaspora families often carry the responsibility of coordinating healthcare for parents and loved ones back home. The distance makes it difficult. Reliable, professional local support is hard to find.

AfriPharma360 was built specifically to bridge that gap — giving families a trusted, coordinated healthcare support service they can count on, no matter where they are in the world.

What AfriPharma360 Provides.

Every AfriPharma360 plan is built around the core services families need most — with more support at higher tiers.

Important: Monthly AfriPharma360 subscriptions cover healthcare coordination, support, and communication services only. Prescription medications, medical consultations, laboratory services, delivery fees, and other healthcare products or services are purchased separately from licensed healthcare providers or pharmacies and are not included in subscription fees.

Medication Coordination

Prescription tracking, refill reminders, and coordination support to help families stay informed and reduce the risk of missed refills.

Appointment Support

Scheduling, reminders, and coordination for doctor visits, specialist appointments, and follow-up care.

Chronic Care Support

Structured support for diabetes, hypertension, HIV/AIDS, asthma, and other long-term conditions.

Escalation Support

When something needs urgent attention, your coordinator escalates to the right professional or facility. Platinum tier.

Care Model & Oversight

Nurse-led coordination, built for trust.

AfriPharma360 is a healthcare coordination and family support service. Your care coordination is led by licensed nurses operating under the supervision of our Medical Director. We do not diagnose or prescribe, we do not sell or dispense medication, and we do not provide emergency response.

We handle sensitive personal and health-related information to provide support. In most cases, we require patient consent to provide coordination and share updates with named family members. Where a patient cannot provide informed consent, we may proceed based on a legally authorized representative or Power of Attorney with documentation. For privacy questions, contact our DPO at dpo@afripharma-health.com.
