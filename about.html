About AfriPharma Health Systems

Building pharmaceutical sovereignty in Africa through WHO-aligned manufacturing and supply resilience

The Challenge

Africa imports 88% of its pharmaceuticals, creating supply chain vulnerabilities, higher costs (20-30% premium over local production), limited access to essential medicines, and economic dependency. The COVID-19 pandemic exposed these critical gaps, highlighting the urgent need for local pharmaceutical manufacturing capacity.

Our Solution

AfriPharma Health Systems is establishing Mozambique's first WHO Prequalification-aligned pharmaceutical manufacturing facility to manufacture 7 WHO Essential Medicines in sterile injectable formats.

WHO GMP Compliance

State-of-the-art facility designed to WHO Good Manufacturing Practice standards from inception, ensuring international quality standards.

21.6M Vials Capacity

Annual production capacity to serve healthcare systems across 15 SADC countries with consistent supply.

Strategic Location

Nacala Special Economic Zone with deep-water port access for efficient regional distribution.

Our Vision

To become Africa's leading pharmaceutical manufacturer, contributing to pharmaceutical sovereignty across the continent, healthcare access for underserved populations, economic development through industrialization, skills development in pharmaceutical manufacturing, and regional trade and SADC integration.

Why Mozambique

  • Strategic gateway to 380M people in SADC
  • Nacala deep-water port for regional distribution
  • Special Economic Zone incentives (10-year tax exemption, duty waivers)
  • Government commitment to pharmaceutical manufacturing
  • Growing regional pharmaceutical market ($3.2B annually, 8-12% CAGR)

Our Commitment

AfriPharma is committed to WHO quality standards, Good Manufacturing Practices (GMP), environmental sustainability, job creation and skills development, and an impact-driven business model that balances financial returns with measurable social outcomes.

Corporate Governance

AfriPharma Health Systems Ltd operates under Mauritius corporate governance framework, ensuring transparency, accountability, and investor protection aligned with international best practices. The company is being formalized under the Mauritius Financial Services Commission (FSC) as a Global Business Company (GBC).
