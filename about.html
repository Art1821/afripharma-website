About AfriPharma

Our Story. Our Mission. Our Commitment to Southern Africa.

Who We Are

Built From the Ground Up
for Southern Africa.

AfriPharma Health Systems was founded by a team of pharmaceutical manufacturing professionals with more than 12 years of experience in sterile injectable manufacturing, quality systems, regulatory affairs, and healthcare operations.

The team became increasingly aware of the structural healthcare access challenges facing Southern Africa: fragmented supply chains, insufficient local distribution infrastructure, and near-total dependence on imported medicines.

AfriPharma was created to help solve those challenges — not through aid, but through business. A practical, commercially sustainable platform that improves medicine access today, while building the infrastructure needed for long-term pharmaceutical self-sufficiency across the SADC region.

Our Mission

To improve healthcare access across Southern Africa through pharmacy services, care coordination, pharmaceutical distribution, and future manufacturing — making quality medicines available to the communities that need them most.

Our Vision

To become the leading integrated healthcare access platform and pharmaceutical manufacturer in Southern Africa — a platform built on operational credibility, regulatory excellence, and a genuine commitment to improving lives.

Our Approach

Distribution first. Manufacturing second. AfriPharma's distribution operations generate the commercial track record and cash flows needed to support future WHO-aligned pharmaceutical manufacturing in the SADC region.
