What Should You Report?

Healthcare professionals, patients, and caregivers should report any concerns about AfriPharma medicines. Your reports help us maintain the highest safety standards.

⚠️

Adverse Drug Reactions (ADRs)

  • Side effects (expected or unexpected)
  • Allergic reactions
  • Serious adverse events
  • Medication errors leading to harm
  • Overdose or misuse
🔍

Product Quality Issues

  • Defective packaging or labeling
  • Suspected counterfeit products
  • Physical defects (discoloration, particles)
  • Damaged or broken containers
  • Incorrect product received
💊

Medication Errors

  • Wrong dose administered
  • Wrong route of administration
  • Confusion due to similar names/packaging
  • Prescription or dispensing errors
  • Administration technique errors
📉

Lack of Efficacy

  • Treatment failure
  • Unexpected lack of effect
  • Disease progression despite treatment
  • Suspected product quality issue
  • Therapy not working as expected

What Happens After You Report?

We take every report seriously. Here's our process to ensure patient safety.

Immediate Acknowledgment

Within 24 hours, we acknowledge receipt of your report and assign a unique reference number.

1
2

Initial Assessment

Within 48 hours, our pharmacovigilance team assesses the report for seriousness and follows up if additional information is needed.

Investigation

We investigate the event, review medical records (with consent), and assess causality between the product and event.

3
4

Regulatory Reporting

Serious events: Reported to ANARME (Mozambique) and WHO within 7-15 days
Non-serious events: Included in periodic safety reports

Action & Follow-up

We take appropriate action (product recall, labeling updates, healthcare professional communications) and provide feedback to reporters when permitted.

5

Important Information

⚠️ For Serious or Life-Threatening Events

Do not use this online form. Please call our 24-hour safety hotline immediately: +258 84 XXX XXXX

Serious events include: death, life-threatening situation, hospitalization, persistent disability, or congenital anomaly.

🔒 Confidentiality & Data Protection

All reports are handled in strict confidence according to GDPR and Mozambique data protection laws. Patient identities are protected. Information is used solely for safety monitoring and regulatory compliance.

✅ Regulatory Reporting

AfriPharma reports adverse events to:

  • ANARME (Autoridade Nacional Reguladora de Medicamentos) - Mozambique
  • WHO Programme for International Drug Monitoring (VigiBase)
  • National regulatory authorities in countries where products are marketed

📋 Healthcare Professionals

Healthcare professionals have a professional obligation to report adverse drug reactions. We encourage all HCPs to report suspected ADRs even if:

  • Causality is uncertain
  • The reaction is already known
  • The event seems minor
